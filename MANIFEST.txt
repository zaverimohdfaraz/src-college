SRCC website: all changes from 20 November 2026 in one package
==============================================================
Unzip over the project root and choose "overwrite all".
This replaces the earlier "srcc-about-pages-update.zip"; you do not need that one.

ONE THING TO DELETE
  src/components/AboutShell.tsx   only if it exists (it came in the earlier zip).
                                  It has been replaced by SectionShell.tsx and will
                                  break the build if left in place.

WHAT CHANGED
  Every menu section now has a separate page for each item:
    About ........................ /about, /about/history, /about/vision-mission,
                                   /about/leadership, /about/trust, /about/values
    Academics .................... /academics, /academics/teaching-and-learning,
                                   /academics/calendar
    Departments .................. /departments + /departments/<commerce|accounts|
                                   information-technology|arts|mathematics>
    Admissions ................... /admissions (with enquiry form),
                                   /admissions/eligibility-and-documents, /admissions/faq
    Campus life .................. /campus-life + culture-and-events, sport-and-fitness,
                                   nss-and-community, beyond-the-classroom,
                                   get-involved, gallery
    Facilities ................... /facilities + library, computer-laboratory,
                                   gymnasium, classrooms-and-hall
    Student support .............. /student-support + scholarships, examinations,
                                   careers, anti-ragging, grievance-redressal,
                                   womens-safety, equal-opportunity
    Institutional information .... /institutional-information + committees,
                                   quality-assurance, best-practices,
                                   mandatory-disclosures, alumni, publications

  Section pages: a side menu lists every page in the section (on phones it is a
         tap-to-open list). The Previous/Next links and the page-card grids at
         the bottom of section pages have been removed.
  Desktop dropdowns (all menus): redesigned, with a photo, red group headings,
         compact links and the current page highlighted. About, Admissions and
         Institutional Information show a one-line description under each link;
         Academics shows each course code with its subject, undergraduate
         courses in two columns.
  Homepage slider on phones: slim progress bars (tap to jump), slide name,
         "1 of 4" and a round pause button. Swipe left/right to change slide;
         scrolling the page no longer triggers it.
  Menus: every link opens its own page. Academics lists all 10 courses.
         Admissions now has a dropdown. Phone menu shows group headings.
  Homepage: course list shows banners; videos added.
  Course pages: duration and eligibility added; fees note instead of placeholder.
  Placeholders: filled everywhere except President, Director, Principal and
                Vice Principal details (left as requested).
  Videos (public/videos): cut from the college's 8-minute video, skipping the
                staff interviews and an editing glitch at 0:50-0:55.
