SRCC website — update package 3 (18 Nov 2026)
=============================================
This package REPLACES the earlier update zips and includes everything in them.
Unzip over your srcc-website folder, overwrite when asked, then push and redeploy.
No new packages, so no npm install is needed.

NEW IN THIS PACKAGE: college-supplied photos (staff photos not used)
- Homepage: slider "Campus life" slide uses the college-ground event photo; the four
  campus-life cards now show the Ayodhyam stage performance, badminton on the ground,
  the NSS plantation drive and an industrial visit; "Inside the college" uses the
  corridor-with-students photo.
- Campus Life page: header photo, all four sections (culture, sport, NSS, industrial visit)
  and a new 10-photo gallery. The NSS placeholder is gone.
- About page header: the new "SHRI RAM COLLEGE" building-front photo.
- The "GPS Map Camera" location stamps were cropped off the NSS and industrial-visit photos.

ALSO INCLUDED (from earlier updates)
- Refined crest logo with the name as sharp text; new favicon and home-screen icon.
- "Shri Ram College of Commerce & Science" everywhere.
- Homepage slider fits its text on phones (no overlap under the header).
- Photos uncover gently on scroll and zoom slightly on hover; photo in "Why Shri Ram College".
